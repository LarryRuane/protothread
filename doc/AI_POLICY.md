# AI Policy

AI tools are welcome here, and were used here: much of 2.0.0 was written with
one, and the commits say so. What matters is not whether a tool was involved
but whether a person chose the work, understands it, and is answerable for it.

If you contribute:

- Be able to explain your change in your own words: what it does, why, and what
  else it touches. If you can't yet, the pull request isn't ready.
- Know the language and the surrounding code well enough that you could have
  written the change yourself.
- Write your own issue and review comments. Don't paste AI output at a
  reviewer. If the AI output is itself the point, say so and explain why it
  matters.
- Don't point an unattended agent at this repository. A person picks the work
  and answers for it.

The same bar applies to me. Commits written with AI assistance carry a
`Co-Authored-By` trailer naming the model, which is disclosure, not a transfer
of responsibility: the human author is responsible for every line, and for
everything that reaches you as prose.

Adapted from [Bitcoin Core's AI policy], which was adapted from
[ripgrep's AI policy], which was adapted from [uv's AI policy].
